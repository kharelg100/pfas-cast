PFAS-CAST v1.1.1

Client-side benchmark screening using the censoring-aware multiple-imputation framework from Kharel et al., Cell Reports Sustainability (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crsus.2026.100893.

Exploratory screening and prioritization only; not a regulatory-compliance determination. Uploaded data are processed locally in the browser and are not transmitted by this tool.

This tool is an exploratory screening companion to Kharel et al., Cell Reports Sustainability (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.crsus.2026.100893. It applies the paper's record-level censored-likelihood MI engine to configurable benchmark screening, but it does not reproduce the national population-weighted headline estimand or determine regulatory compliance. Verify current benchmark status before use. No external JavaScript libraries are loaded.
